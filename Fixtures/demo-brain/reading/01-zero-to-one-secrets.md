# Zero to One — monopoly and secrets

Competition copies. A monopoly builds something new.

**A secret** is a truth you can see that others miss — סוד that compounds when you act on it.

## Checklist
- [x] Prefer monopoly over competition
- [x] Hunt for secrets, not trends
- [ ] Be the last mover in the niche

- Start with a small market you can own
- Expand only after the wedge works
- Last-mover advantage beats first-mover hype
