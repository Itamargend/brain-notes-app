# The Prince — virtù and fortuna

Machiavelli studies power **as it is kept**, not as idealism wishes it.

- [x] Arms and law found a state; arms come first
- [x] Virtù is skill, foresight, and adaptability
- [ ] Fortuna is the flood — build dykes early
- Realism over idealism
- Study men as they are, not as they ought to be
